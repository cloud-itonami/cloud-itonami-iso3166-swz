# Business model — SWZ

Independent public-sector market-entry compliance for Eswatini.

- Public Procurement Act No. 7 of 2011 (own Government Gazette text,
  downloaded directly from ESPPRA's own site and read in full — see
  `src/marketentry/facts.cljc`) / Eswatini Public Procurement
  Regulatory Agency (ESPPRA, esppra.co.sz, own text: 'officially
  established on the 27th May 2013', 'Category A public enterprise,
  administratively, reporting to the Ministry of Finance')
- Registrar of Companies (Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Trade) —
  business/company registration through the Government Online Services
  e-portal (online.gov.sz, own text: 'Company Name Search / Company
  Name Reservation / Company Registration'); the Eswatini Investment
  Promotion Authority (EIPA) facilitates registration for foreign/local
  investors and names the Registrar of Companies directly in its own
  Start A Business requirements
- Eswatini Revenue Service (ERS) Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN)
  registration (Revenue Authority Act No. 1 of 2008) and VAT
  registration (Value Added Tax Act, 2011 — currently E900,000 annual
  taxable turnover per ERS's own stated operative threshold; the Act's
  own text delegates the exact figure to a Gazette notice)
- Public Procurement Act No. 7 of 2011 s.40(1)(g)/55/56 supplier-
  eligibility gate — bars an engagement from a clean filing/submit
  when its own claimed eligibility self-certification does not survive
  the Act's own disqualification grounds (a discretionary ESPPRA
  suspension, or an automatic Register for Tender Defaulters
  endorsement under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 2006)

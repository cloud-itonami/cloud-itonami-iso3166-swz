# Operator guide — SWZ

Portal: ESPPRA (Eswatini Public Procurement Regulatory Agency,
esppra.co.sz) — the Public Procurement Act No. 7 of 2011 is the named
legal basis (own Government Gazette text, downloaded directly and read
in full this iteration). ESPPRA's own 'About Us' page (own text,
fetched directly) confirms it was 'officially established on the 27th
May 2013, following the coming into force of the Public Procurement
Act No. 7 of 2011'.

Entity: Registrar of Companies (Ministry of Commerce, Industry and
Trade), business/company registration through the Government Online
Services e-portal (online.gov.sz). The Eswatini Investment Promotion
Authority (EIPA) facilitates registration for foreign/local investors
and requires Trading License applications to include 'Company
formation documents certified by the registrar of companies
(Memorandum and Articles of Association, Form J and Certificate of
Incorporation)'. Tax registration (Taxpayer Identification Number,
VAT) runs through the Eswatini Revenue Service (ERS).

Engagements filing/submitting through this actor also clear the Act's
own s.40(1)(g)/55/56 supplier-eligibility gate: an engagement's own
claimed eligibility self-certification must actually survive both of
the Act's disqualification grounds (no active ESPPRA suspension, no
automatic Register for Tender Defaulters endorsement) before a filing
can proceed. Human-gated filing only — `:filing/draft`/`:filing/submit`
never auto-commit, at any rollout phase.
